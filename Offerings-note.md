# Offerings Note

## Current front-runner — personalized AI practice / stack

Walk-talk 2026-06-15 broadened the offer from one briefing workflow into a
clearer umbrella:

> Help knowledge workers compose their own personalized AI stack/practice for
> messy knowledge work, self-discovery, and live projects generic AI cannot
> understand out of the box.

The key claim: no one can personalize this *for* the user. Bench & Beacon can
coach the build, demonstrate lived examples, and sell products that lower the
friction, but the durable value comes from the user's own input + curation
flywheel. The stack has three layers:

1. personal read-only source of truth;
2. shared human-AI managed working layer;
3. outsourced production tooling underneath.

Commercial proof points become **live projects** rather than separate offerings:
weekly briefing automation, voice workflow, Ben B as co-presenter,
self-discovery / interest audit, and health-regime nudges. Source:
[[COUNSEL/walk-talk/2026-06-15-walk-1/summary]].

Evening refinement from [[COUNSEL/walk-talk/2026-06-15-walk-2/summary]]: do not
try to perfect the offer at the desk. The offer is discovered by shipping: two
posts/week plus near-term video reps expose what lands, while
[[CRAFT/Post-Craft/posts/material/durable-ai-observations|Durable AI Observations]]
becomes the repeatable thesis bank.

2026-06-16 live candidate inside this umbrella:
**freeing attention by helping people outsource discipline with intention.** The
walk deliberately broke the narrower "AI advisory" frame: the useful buyer pain
may be discipline/attention, with AI as the leverage layer rather than the thing
being sold. Open fork: stress-test this against the pragmatist avatar. If it
doesn't survive as an offer, keep it as a writing thesis. Source:
[[COUNSEL/walk-talk/2026-06-16-walk-1/summary]].

2026-06-17 boundary: the private signal/attention system now belongs to
`VENTURE/Sand-Map/attention-agent/`. Bench & Beacon can use public-safe outputs
as examples, posts, or session prompts, but should not treat the attention agent
itself as a client-facing product.

2026-06-17 walk refinement: the sell motion is not "AI advisory" or a
methodology pitch; it is solving many small real problems with an externalized
knowledge base plus agents, then using those examples to convert people by
showing the process. Kenny chose the harder path of reaching a broader audience,
not only buyers already sold on externalizing. Differentiator: **intelligence
for flourishing, not extraction**. Next step: collect concrete use cases.
Source: [[COUNSEL/walk-talk/2026-06-17-walk-1/summary]].

## First concrete slice — weekly briefing automation

Walk-talk 2026-06-14 walk-3 collapsed the Bench & Beacon offer to:

> Build your weekly briefing automation so you consume smarter, not more.

Shape: use Kenny's own AtlasCortex / Sand Map attention-agent / briefing workflow
as the proof of concept, then point only the cleaned briefing slice at a specific
buyer pain: too much AI/news/research intake, not enough usable synthesis. First
concrete delivery vehicle: Friday session #1, a walkthrough of the briefing
build.

2026-06-15 refinement: keep this as the first worked example inside the broader
personalized-stack offer, not the whole business. Next commercial step: turn the
umbrella offer into one line (problem × buyer × outcome), then use the briefing
slice as the most concrete paid ask before July 13.
