## The north star — the offer

Generic, off-the-shelf AI is sycophantic and one-size-fits-all; it structurally
can't do real self-discovery or deep knowledge work. The Bench & Beacon offer is
helping people **compose their own personalized AI stack** — a read-only personal
vault → a shared human-AI managed layer → outsourced production tooling. Two legs:

1. **Coaching/consulting** people through building their own system (no one can
   personalize it *for* them).
2. **Products** that lower the friction of that build.

The standing bottleneck is that there's no one-line offer yet. **Next step:** write
it as problem × buyer × outcome. **Milestone:** first paying dollar **by July 13**.
Evening refinement: ship first, then let response narrow the offer.

## Appendix — the retired "becoming the harness" ladder

The pre-walk design framed this as a 5-rung teaching ladder (First contact → AI
in the flow of work → Thinking partner → Agents that execute → You are the
harness), personas as entry points rather than parallel tracks. Walk-1 retired
the syllabus framing in favor of the projects roadmap above. The ladder survives
as a **lens** — a way to place where a given project meets its audience (e.g. the
weekly-briefing build lives around "thinking partner"; the Codex material around
"agents that execute"; "you are the harness" stays the thesis, kept as a
destination, not a doorway). The full original design is in the [[VENTURE/business-dev/bench-beacon/_dev/Beginnings of creating a curriculum|_dev draft]].
