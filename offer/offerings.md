# Offerings Note

## Current front-runner — personalized AI practice / stack

Walk-talk 2026-06-15 broadened the offer from one briefing workflow into a
clearer umbrella:

> Help knowledge workers compose their own personalized AI stack/practice for
> messy knowledge work, self-discovery, and live projects generic AI cannot
> understand out of the box.

The key claim: no one can personalize this *for* the user. Bench & Beacon can
coach the build, demonstrate lived examples, and sell products that lower the
friction, but the durable value comes from the user's own input + curation
flywheel. The stack has three layers:

1. personal read-only source of truth;
2. shared human-AI managed working layer;
3. outsourced production tooling underneath.

Commercial proof points become **live projects** rather than separate offerings:
weekly briefing automation, voice workflow, BenBe as co-presenter,
self-discovery / interest audit, and health-regime nudges. Source:
[[COUNSEL/walk-talk/2026-06-15/summary#Walk 1]].

## 2026-06-23 working lists — video-chat domains and skill inventory

This is not final offer copy. It is a working chooser list for video chats and
a grouped inventory of Bench & Beacon capabilities.

### Services Offered During Video Chats

- **AI Fluency.** Learn how to use AI tools well on real tasks, not toy prompts.
- **AI Workspace Setup.** Set up notes, files, prompts, projects, and context so
  AI can work with actual material.
- **Domain Exploration.** Get oriented in an unfamiliar field, market,
  technology, company category, or body of knowledge.
- **Research & Briefing.** Turn scattered information into a clearer map, source
  list, briefing, or decision surface.
- **Technical / Science Understanding.** Make sense of technical docs,
  scientific claims, AI tools, health topics, or expert debates.
- **Automation & Tool Building.** Find where a small app, script, agent,
  dashboard, or workflow could remove recurring friction.
- **Finance / Operations Literacy.** Understand runway, burn, bookkeeping shape,
  entity/admin structure, or basic operating numbers. Not tax/accounting advice.
- **Attention & Workflow Systems.** Improve capture, review, priorities,
  recurring loops, and information hygiene.

### Bench And Beacon Skill Inventory

1. **AI Systems & Agents**
   - Frontier model workflows
   - Coding agents
   - Research and analysis agents
2. **Local Tooling & Automation Engineering**
   - Command-line tools, scripts, and wrappers
   - MCP adapters and tool registries
   - Automated capture, sync, and reporting workflows
3. **Knowledge Architecture**
   - Obsidian vaults and source-of-truth systems
   - Personal and company knowledge bases
   - Retrieval, review, and artifact hygiene
4. **App & Prototype Development**
   - Small web apps and internal tools
   - Dashboards and workflow prototypes
   - Agent-assisted software development
5. **Research & Synthesis**
   - Market maps and company teardowns
   - Source evaluation and briefing systems
   - Unfamiliar-domain exploration
6. **Finance & Operations Systems**
   - Runway, burn, and cost tracking
   - Entity, admin, and document organization
   - CPA/bookkeeper/vendor handoff systems
7. **Toolchain Evaluation & Integration**
   - Testing new agent-facing tools
   - Evaluating security and permission posture
   - Integrating APIs, MCP servers, local apps, and hosted services
8. **Technical-Business Translation**
   - Translating technical systems into practical options
   - Connecting tools, operations, finance, and strategy
   - Turning ambiguous problems into next actions
