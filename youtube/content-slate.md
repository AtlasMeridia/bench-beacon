# Content Slate

The channel should present real Bench & Beacon projects as they develop. It is a chronological archive of live work, not a course catalog.

## Positioning

Core claim:

Generic AI is not enough for messy knowledge work, self-discovery, and live projects. The durable advantage is a personalized AI stack with a source of truth, a human-AI working layer, and production tooling underneath.

Audience:

Knowledge workers, builders, operators, and serious generalists who want practical AI systems that free attention and improve judgment.

Differentiator:

Intelligence for flourishing, not extraction. The channel should show practical systems that help a person think and act better, not just squeeze more output from them.

Format:

- Show a live project.
- Name the principle.
- Demonstrate the artifact.
- Extract one practical action.
- Point to the next iteration.

## Pilot Episode

Working title:

`The model was never the bottleneck`

Thumbnail claim:

`Build the flywheel`

Viewer promise:

By the end, the viewer understands why the input and curation loop matters more than model choice, and sees a concrete weekly publishing system built from it.

Artifact to show:

Bench & Beacon operating loop: walk-talk -> notes -> AI synthesis -> deck -> public artifact -> followup.

CTA:

If you are trying to turn scattered inputs into a weekly briefing or working knowledge system, Bench & Beacon is the live build surface for that.

## Selection Rules

Choose the next video only when it has:

- one sharp claim;
- one visible artifact;
- one viewer promise;
- one thumbnail claim;
- one next action.

Kill or defer ideas that only have a vibe, a topic, or a tool tour.

## Weekly Derivatives

For each Friday video:

- Tuesday post: the practical how.
- Thursday post: the broader why.
- Optional Short: one strong moment from the transcript.
- Followup: one note about what the video revealed about the format or offer.
- Signal log: one note about concrete buyer language, use cases, objections, or paid-ask responses.
