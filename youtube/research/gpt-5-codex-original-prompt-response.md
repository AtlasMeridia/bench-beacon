## Bottom Line

Start a weekly Bench & Beacon video as a produced "live project dispatch," not a generic YouTube channel. The strongest format is:

`12-18 minute Friday video = on-camera thesis + iA Presenter deck + screen/demo footage + a concrete artifact`

Then derive the Tue/Thu posts from the same weekly idea. This matches the walk-talk direction: video is the main artifact, writing is downstream, and the first-dollar offer is helping knowledge workers build practical AI systems that free attention.

## Weekly Video Checklist

Monday: choose thesis, viewer promise, artifact to show, and thumbnail claim.

Tuesday: publish short written post from the thesis.

Wednesday: make iA deck, record demo/screen sections, collect B-roll.

Thursday: record A-roll, create 3 title/thumbnail variants, publish second written post.

Friday: edit, export, upload, schedule/publish, pin comment.

Saturday: record metrics: CTR, average view duration, retention drop-off, comments, title/thumb notes.

Sunday: pick next week's candidate.

## Per-Video Structure

Use this every week until it breaks:

1. Cold open, 0:00-0:15: one sharp claim.
2. Promise, 0:15-0:45: what the viewer will be able to do or see.
3. Context, 0:45-2:00: why this matters now.
4. Demonstration, 2:00-8:00: show the system/artifact.
5. Interpretation, 8:00-13:00: what this reveals.
6. Next action, 13:00-16:00: one concrete thing to try.
7. Close: abrupt, no long outro.
