## Video Structure

| Section | Target time | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Cold open | 0:00 to 0:15 | Make one sharp claim. |
| Promise | 0:15 to 0:45 | Say what the viewer will see or be able to do. |
| Context | 0:45 to 2:00 | Explain why this matters now. |
| Demonstration | 2:00 to 8:00 | Show the system, artifact, or workflow. |
| Interpretation | 8:00 to 13:00 | Explain what the artifact reveals. |
| Next action | 13:00 to 16:00 | Give one concrete thing to try. |
| Close | final seconds | End cleanly without a long outro. |

## Thumbnail Spec

- Canvas: 1280x720.
- Working file can be larger, but export at YouTube thumbnail size.
- Text: 2 to 4 words.
- Typeface: Cormorant Garamond or another established Atlas display face.
- Colors: navy, cream, gold.
- Image: real face, screen artifact, or clean diagram.
- Mark: small Bench & Beacon bug in one corner.
- Avoid: gradients, stock imagery, dense UI collages, AI portrait identity.

## Thumbnail Variants

Create two variants when possible:

1. Face plus claim.
2. Artifact or diagram plus claim.

Treat face vs. no-face and serif vs. bold sans as early tests, not taste debates.
